How the course works

Learn, check, apply and keep evidence

Every two-week module has three substantive theory sections, source-grounded checks and written evidence.

1. Read and inspect

Use the supplied sources, project plan and current Stage 4 Technology ideas to build accurate understanding.

2. Practise

Use the knowledge checks and feedback to correct misconceptions.

3. Apply

Connect the theory to your own observations and decisions in the project.

4. Review and save

Use Print / Save PDF and the folio backup to keep evidence outside the webpage.

Student evidence. Written evidence autosaves on this browser and device. Browser storage is a working copy, not cloud submission.

Core project resource

Programmable Lamp drawing

How to use this plan

Read the title block, scale labels, dimension lines and three cut-order labels before deciding what the drawing communicates. Use the full PDF for detail rather than measuring this preview.

  • The byte-preserved root drawing is the authority for its visible geometry and labels; the Week 02 delivery copy is textually identical.
  • The separate tool-and-material worksheet identifies radiata pine, 3 mm clear acrylic, wood screws, Arduino Uno, RGB LED strip and related items.
  • Units, material assignment to each shape, tolerances, electronics placement and construction method are not confirmed by the drawing alone.
Teacher to confirm: exact units, stock preparation, component assignments, approved sequence and every practical setting.

Syllabus outcomes

Technology 7-8 Syllabus (2023) · Stage 4

This is the governing NSW syllabus authority, implemented from 2026. The eight outcomes provide the current mapping frame; which website or folio evidence is formally assessed remains Teacher to confirm.

  • TE4-SDP-01 explains relationships between sustainability, design and production
  • TE4-PDP-01 describes the practices and processes of designers and producers
  • TE4-MSC-01 explains how materials, systems and components contribute to solutions
  • TE4-PPM-01 applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects
  • TE4-DES-01 communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
  • TE4-SAF-01 selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes
  • TE4-DIG-01 demonstrates technological literacy to safely interact in digital environments
  • TE4-DIG-02 uses data and digital systems to code, design and produce projects
