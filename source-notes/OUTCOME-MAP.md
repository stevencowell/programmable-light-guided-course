# Programmable Light outcome and evidence map

Governing syllabus: **NESA Technology 7-8 Syllabus (2023)**, implemented from 2026. This map identifies learning opportunities and student evidence; it does not make the website or folio a formal assessment task.

| Outcome | Source-grounded learning opportunity | Observable student evidence | Formal status boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TE4-SDP-01** | Timber sourcing, forest management, sustainability, design and production relationships | Materials/sustainability reasoning; final sustainability reflection | Which evidence is formally assessed is Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-PDP-01** | Image research, idea generation, iterative comparison and producer practices | Attributed research; six ideas; selected-design justification | Final criteria and feasibility approval are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-MSC-01** | Roles of timber, acrylic, Arduino Uno, RGB LED strip and other confirmed components | Tool/material/component explanations; reasoned material decision; system explanation | Circuit, placement and performance specifications are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-PPM-01** | Plan reading, proposed production planning, checkpoints and evidence management | Drawing interpretation; cautious sequence; dated production/problem/test record | Weeks 4-10 practical order and sign-off points are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-DES-01** | Communicating, comparing and evaluating ideas and solutions | Six ideas; selected-design reasoning; annotated evidence; final evaluation | Class-agreed criteria and formal judgement arrangements are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-SAF-01** | Hazard/risk/control reasoning; training, permission and observed safe use | Safety response; teacher-controlled machine-access checkpoints; safe production notes | The website never grants equipment permission; local procedure and PPE are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-DIG-01** | Technological literacy, ethical image research and safe digital participation | Creator/site/URL/reuse notes; responsible use explanation | Approved imagery and Classroom destination are Teacher to confirm. |
| **TE4-DIG-02** | Vector layers, algorithms, sequencing, variables, loops, conditionals and system planning | Two-layer practice record; pseudocode/flowchart; observable test/retest evidence | Wiring, pins, libraries, code and upload process are Teacher to confirm. |

## Five-module evidence pathway

| Module | Weeks | Three-section emphasis | Folio connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Project purpose and components; workshop safety; measurement and supplied drawing | Cards 01-04 |
| 2 | 3-4 | Image quality/research; vector layers and practice; idea iteration and criteria | Cards 05-08 |
| 3 | 5-6 | Timber groups/material reasoning; forest types/sustainability; cautious production planning | Cards 09 and 11 |
| 4 | 7-8 | Coding and digital-system concepts from the supplied coding theory; project-specific implementation remains teacher-controlled | Card 10 |
| 5 | 9-10 | Source-grounded testing, evidence communication and evaluation; the absent practical sequence remains teacher-controlled | Cards 11-12 |
