## Semantic acceptance rules for integration

- The real plan PDF/PNG is the only plan authority. Generated visual 08 is contextual and must not substitute for it.
- Generated electronics scenes show component roles and system relationships only. They must not be used to teach pin assignments, voltage, wiring, power arrangement, exact code, timing or approved practical procedure.
- Generated project forms are illustrative. They do not establish the final design, acrylic geometry, timber stock size, joints, fit or student evidence.
- Supplied worksheets may be shown as authorised activity records. Blank cells, prompts and example images must not be misrepresented as completed facts or separately licensed media.
- Machine work requires the official SOP, teacher instruction and local controls. A PPE photograph or generated scene is never a complete risk-control record.
- Every missing technical or teacher-controlled detail remains **Teacher to confirm**.
