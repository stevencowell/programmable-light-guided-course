## 7. Safety and dependency boundaries

1. The website and programme do not grant permission to use any equipment.
2. Current school WHS procedures, ESIS, teacher demonstrations, training and supervision override website wording.
3. Fixed disc-sander use requires the current authorised controls, a teacher demonstration, the required safety test, teacher permission and observed safe use. Local PPE, emergency-stop and first-aid arrangements are **Teacher to confirm**.
4. Laser-cutter workflow, file checking, authorised material, ventilation, supervision and settings are **Teacher to confirm**. The key-tag activity is software practice, not machine authorisation.
5. Soldering/electronics work must not begin from this programme alone. Approved circuitry, component ratings, power arrangement, connections and local safety procedure are **Teacher to confirm**.
6. The supplied drawing must be opened at full size. A screen preview must not be measured, and unclear units or feature relationships must be referred to the teacher.
7. Weeks 4–10 practical content is a documented dependency gap. A teacher-approved job sequence/checkpoint record must be added before practical delivery is represented in the programme.
8. Student photos and researched images require current school privacy, copyright, reuse and publishing decisions. Public website access is not consent.
9. The optional executable is excluded pending local security and curriculum approval.
